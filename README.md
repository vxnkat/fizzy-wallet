## 📌 About the Project

**Fizzy Prime** is a personal finance and productivity dashboard concept
focused on making everyday tracking feel simple and approachable. Its
clean interface brings key information together so users can quickly
review their financial progress, check their schedule, and stay
consistent with their goals.

The dashboard uses a soft lavender visual theme, rounded cards, subtle
shadows, and clear visual indicators to keep the experience lightweight
and easy to navigate.

## ✨ Features

-   **Card Balance** --- A quick view of the current balance.
-   **Overall Progress** --- A visual summary of income, expenses,
    budget usage, and goals.
-   **Today's Schedule** --- A dedicated area for planned payments and
    upcoming items.
-   **Logging Streak** --- A simple way to track consistency.
-   **Quick Actions** --- Shortcuts to frequently used app sections.
-   **Bottom Navigation** --- Easy access to the main areas of the app.
-   **Settings Access** --- A dedicated entry point for app preferences.

> Feature availability may vary depending on the current implementation.

## 🎨 Design Highlights

-   Soft lavender and white colour palette
-   Minimal, card-based dashboard layout
-   Rounded components and subtle depth
-   Clear typography and compact information hierarchy
